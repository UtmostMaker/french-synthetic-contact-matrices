# Decision Log

## D0001
- Date: 2026-04-15
- Status: accepted
- Decision: Start with a durable local-first research framework before selecting a final paper idea.
- Rationale: The project is intended to run over days or weeks and must survive interruptions without losing context, reproducibility, or experimental traceability.
- Consequences:
  - More upfront structure.
  - Lower risk of chaos during experimentation and drafting.
  - Easier final packaging for GitHub and arXiv.

## D0002
- Date: 2026-04-15
- Status: accepted
- Decision: Pivot from a narrow epidemiology-first target toward a venue-aligned bilingual publication strategy: French for ROIA, English for arXiv, with one shared scientific core.
- Rationale: ROIA explicitly welcomes French-language AI research and interdisciplinary work with the social sciences and humanities, which broadens the contribution space without wasting the existing reproducibility framework.
- Consequences:
  - The framework remains valid.
  - The topic selection criterion now explicitly includes ROIA fit.
  - Scientific assets must support two publication tracks, not one.

## D0003
- Date: 2026-04-15
- Status: accepted
- Decision: Expand the shortlist into a 20-idea scored landscape and keep IDEA-0001 as the main candidate with IDEA-0002 as the backup.
- Rationale: IDEA-0001 offers the best ROIA differentiation while remaining salvageable into IDEA-0002 because both can share the same France-first data pipeline, baselines, and evaluation spine.

  - The next work package should focus on a shared experimental backbone, not additional brainstorming.

## D0004
- Date: 2026-04-15
- Status: accepted
- Decision: Prioritize ROIA fit over arXiv optimization during topic selection and narrow the active shortlist accordingly.
- Rationale: ROIA's French-language AI plus SSH scope rewards topics that connect method, behavior, trust, territorial context, or representational critique more directly than a generic method paper would.
- Consequences:
  - IDEA-0001 remains the main candidate.
  - IDEA-0002 remains the backup because it is the most defensible and reusable fallback.
  - arXiv becomes a secondary packaging target for now, not the main selection criterion.
